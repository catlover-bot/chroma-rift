# 第一章「最後の退館者」 — audio direction

The building has nearly finished its closing routine. Music leaves enough air for a distant step, a handle tooth, and a key to become useful information. The 巡回体 is conveyed through floor contact and restrained cloth/metal movement, not an unrelated alarm bed. Silence after containment is part of the resolution.

The original four-note call is **D4–A4–E4–F4**. Its minor third leaves the title unanswered; the ending answers **D4–A4–F♯4–D4**. The score uses performed upright-piano, cello, pizzicato, harp and struck-metal samples. Each piece has explicit notes, varied phrases, cadences, short room tails and rests. This is a newly authored composition; no existing melody, MIDI, song or musical loop was used.

| Prepared piece | Length | Function and form |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [title_theme](../assets/audio/music/title_theme.m4a) | 54 s | Solo call, cello response, displaced harmony, thinning return |
| [exploration](../assets/audio/music/exploration.m4a) | 88 s | Five sparse four-bar rooms; alternate piano and harp answers |
| [suspicion](../assets/audio/music/suspicion.m4a) | 56 s | Three-beat fragments, semitone shade, metal echo, full breaths |
| [pursuit](../assets/audio/music/pursuit.m4a) | 38 s | Compressed motif and varied pizzicato pulse with two-beat gaps |
| [release](../assets/audio/music/release.m4a) | 10 s | A finite answered call, open fifth and long air |
| [chapter_end](../assets/audio/music/chapter_end.m4a) | 62 s | Title recalled, harmony opens, major answer, final decay and silence |

The first four pieces loop as complete prepared mixes with deliberate quiet seams. They are not phase-sensitive runtime stems. Release and ending play once per request; repeated state publication does not restart them. Minimum residence and candidate-state delays prevent brief threat changes from retriggering cues. Crossfades use at most two music voices and a 1.6-second linear gain transition. Actual containment retires pursuit immediately; release has time to finish before ordinary exploration returns. Backgrounding, pause, mute, failed GL sessions and disposal stop owned music. Resume requires a current state request and does not catch up elapsed background time.

The scene owns ten prepared effect/environment voices and at most two music voices. Area-specific clips reuse those slots: handle grip, key/lock, ratchet, bronze bell, sliding isolation seal, power relay, two footstep variations, cloth/metal movement and one room/outdoor environment. These new physical cues are original deterministic noise/modal models, not recorded Foley. Each indoor area has a different restrained air/settling texture. Outdoor wind replaces the room voice. The earlier areas keep their existing short door-impact clip because their completion tails are shorter than the new area-05 isolation slide. Position affects attenuation only; no unsupported native 3D panning or reverb API is assumed. Important mechanism sounds duck music; footsteps remain below the selected effect level and never raise user volume.

`musicVolume`, `effectsVolume`, and `environmentVolume` are independent. Legacy `musicVolume` had controlled the room bed, so missing `environmentVolume` inherits that saved value, including explicit zero. Mute stays mute. The existing optional-native-module guard, playback-only audio mode, silent-switch behavior, microphone prohibition and background-playback prohibition remain in place. The title/credits owner uses `musicOnly` and allocates no effect pool.

## Rebuilding and provenance

The editable source is [chapter-one-score.json](../scripts/audio/chapter-one-score.json), generated by [compose-score.py](../scripts/audio/compose-score.py). The pinned [instrument manifest](../scripts/audio/instrument-sources.json) lists every original URL, SHA-256, source commit, root pitch and license. Eight isolated samples (16.55 MB cached outside the app) were taken from [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/tree/440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e), under [CC0-1.0](../scripts/audio/VSCO-CC0-LICENSE.txt). Credits: Sam Gossner / Versilian Studios and Simon Dalzell / Ivy Audio; sample editing by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. Only the six compressed final mixes ship, about 3.3 MB; the source library is not bundled. Pitch roots were checked against the repository's pinned SFZ mappings rather than inferred from filenames.

Offline tools are Python 3, NumPy and FFmpeg. They are authoring tools, not app dependencies. From the repository root:

```sh
python3 scripts/audio/compose-score.py
python3 scripts/audio/render-chapter-music.py --cache /tmp/chroma-vsco-small
python3 scripts/audio/render-physical-sounds.py
```

The music renderer fetches only missing pinned samples and verifies their hashes. It decodes samples, changes pitch, gates individual notes, renders short stereo room taps, performs two-pass loudness normalization and encodes AAC-LC at 44.1 kHz / 112 kbit/s. Physical cues use small 24 kHz mono PCM files. Reproduction requires compatible FFmpeg/NumPy versions; the analysis manifests record the exact shipped output hashes. No runtime buffer creation or per-frame synthesis is used. Installed `expo-audio` 57.0.5 source and the [official Expo Audio API](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/audio/) were checked for `isLoaded`, `volume`, `loop`, `play`, `pause`, asynchronous `seekTo`, and owned-player release. No sample-accurate start scheduling is assumed.

## Verification and listening status

[Music measurements](qa-goal014/audio/music-analysis.json) refer to the **decoded compressed assets**: all six are approximately −22 LUFS integrated, true peaks below −4 dBTP, no clipped samples, and no material mono cancellation. [Physical measurements](qa-goal014/audio/physical-analysis.json) give duration, peak, DC, RMS, loop seam and hash for each cue. The music's strong file-level headroom helps preserve space for steps and mechanisms, but these measurements do not establish the peak of every simultaneous native mix or the listener's sound pressure level.

Automated owner/director tests cover bounded voice ownership, state hysteresis, safe-state priority, one-shot completion, ducking, mute/zero, late loading, stale sessions, failure cleanup, and ten repeated entries/disposals. They test calls and ownership, not heard output.

**Status: technical mix candidate.** Artistic listening, headphone/speaker balance, and the actual iPhone mix remain pending. File LUFS/peak results are not a hearing-safety guarantee. `DEVICE_ACCEPTANCE=PENDING`; `RELEASE_READY=false`. Listening acceptance must check the title theme identity, distinct pursuit energy, a meaningful release of tension, ending resolution and decay, footstep audibility, scene-to-scene transitions, mute/suspend behavior, and mono playback at a comfortable device volume.

## Controller route and listening sequence

The [controller route mix](qa-goal014/audio/controller-route.m4a) reconstructs 150.684 seconds of a fresh standard-intensity five-area route. The extractor runs the actual natural controller commands and campaign handoffs, then the actual `presentChapterAudio` and `flushControllerAudioFrame` hooks. It reached containment, isolation, power stop and outdoor exit, including real exploration, suspicion, pursuit and release music decisions. Maximum ownership was 12 players; every player was released at the end.

This is **offline command-trace reconstruction**, not a recording of an iPhone or Expo player output. Accepted presentation and already-loaded players with immediate seek completion are explicit fixtures. [The report](qa-goal014/audio/controller-audio-report.json) records those limits, current loaded-source/asset hashes, state transitions and actual played-source counts. [The complete compressed command trace](qa-goal014/audio/controller-recording.json.gz) preserves every player command, volume change and area-local simulation timestamp.

The separate [listening sequence](qa-goal014/audio/listening-sequence.m4a) requests exploration → suspicion → pursuit → release → chapter_end through the actual audio owner, with footsteps, bell, isolation and outdoor sound. Its state sequence is authored for audition and is not represented as played gameplay. The game owner is disposed before a separate `musicOnly` owner starts `chapter_end`, using the same configuration and state calls as `ChapterMusic.tsx`; the complete ending decay and silence are included. A separate [16-second ending/credits excerpt](qa-goal014/audio/ending-intro.m4a) supplies 12 seconds of intro plus 4 seconds of credits for the labeled visual replay. This excerpt intentionally stops before the full 62-second composition ends, and does not claim a React/native audio capture. Both reconstructed mixes retain the actual default sliders, with no normalization boost; measured integrated loudness is approximately −37 LUFS and true peak below −19 dBTP. [Decoded mix measurements](qa-goal014/audio/mix-analysis.json) include mono compatibility and clipping checks. Individual music links above provide the prepared −22 LUFS composition files for listening independently of quiet gameplay sliders.

The [before mix](qa-goal014/audio/before/route-video-aligned.m4a) uses the genuine old owner from commit `3632675e5c42ac252cca433a5e6dd45bb028f8c5`, extracted in an isolated git archive with the installed dependencies shared by symlink. It contains the original effects and ambience and adds no invented music to the baseline. All 108 loaded source/input hashes were compared to the archived commit. [The before report](qa-goal014/audio/before/controller-audio-report.json) records the exact existing video/frame-map hashes and explicit playback/presentation fixtures. Its output is aligned to that video's 5 Hz samples and retains original default volumes, measuring −43.05 LUFS / −22.99 dBTP. Differences in these measurements describe the offline mixes; they do not establish a listener's preferred loudness or a native-device quality judgment.
